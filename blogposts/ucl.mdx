Alongside the EPL, the following competitions make up the traditional top tier of global football

- <HighlightText>La Liga (Spain)</HighlightText>: Known for its technical play and historic rivalries, featuring legendary clubs like Real Madrid and FC Barcelona.

- <HighlightText>Serie A (Italy)</HighlightText>: Renowned for its rich tactical history and passionate fan culture.

- <HighlightText>Bundesliga (Germany)</HighlightText>: Celebrated for its fiercely loyal fanbases, high-scoring matches, and intense stadium atmospheres.

- <HighlightText>Ligue 1 (France)</HighlightText>: Home to some of Europe's top clubs and emerging global talent.
