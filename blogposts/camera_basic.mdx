## Lens

Take the **Canon RF 100mm F2.8L MACRO IS USM** for example:

 - `RF` = the lens type, corresponding to the different types of lens mount on Canon cameras.
 - `100mm` = the focal length of the lens.
 - `F2.8` = the maximum aperture.
 - `L` means this lens is one of Canon's L-series "professional" (higher-quality) range.
 - `MACRO` = includes the capability to capture images at macro magnification.
 - `IS` = the lens has a built-in optical Image Stabilizer to reduce blurring caused by camera shake.
 - `USM` = Ultra Sonic Motor, a fast and quiet focusing motor.

## Terms

### The Crop Factor

Because the sensor is smaller, it captures only the central portion

<CustomImage src="crop_factor.jpg" />

For example, a 50mm lens on a 1.5x crop sensor behaves like a 75mm lens

### Full stop

Is a standard unit of measurement in photography that represents the doubling or halving of the amount of light reaching the camera's sensor.

<CustomImage
  src="full_stop.jpg"
/>

### Fast lens

A fast lens is a lens with a large maximum aperture (a small f-number, such as f/1.4, f/1.8, or f/2.8) that lets in a high volume of light, allows you to use a faster shutter speed.
